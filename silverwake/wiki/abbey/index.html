The abbey and its bells

Every sector was once a working part of a city built to serve the dead. Without a dawn, each one kept doing its job and went wrong doing it. That is why the creatures attack you: they are the Wake's servants, still serving.

The ten Wake Bells

Ten bells, each tuned to hold a share of moonlight and rung together to hold the night in place. Each is kept by a Guardian. When a Guardian falls its bell cracks, and small lights rise and drift toward the sea.

The Guardians are not villains

Each one is a mourner that cannot stop mourning, under one last order: "Let no light leave." Most of their last words thank you. Silencing them is mercy as well as duty.

The Wakeful

The hunter that comes when a bearer lingers too long in a sector is the Wake's patience given a shape. It has no lines.
