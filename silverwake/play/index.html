Controls

On a phone or tablet, drag anywhere to fly and tap to dash, as in the app. With a keyboard:

FlyW A S D, or drag with the mouse
AimArrow keys (the gun fires by itself in a fight)
DashShift, or click
ParryC
Melee strikeV
Swap gunsF
ItemE
BombQ
Slow motionSpace
PauseEsc

A gamepad works too: left stick to fly, right stick to aim, B to dash.

The browser chapter has no co-op and no store. Sectors 4-10, two-phone co-op, Google Play Games achievements and leaderboards are in the Android app.
