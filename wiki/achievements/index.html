Achievements

40 achievements are in the game today, on the Achievements screen in the Boss Arena. More are planned, along with achievements on Google Play. This list shows both, and each entry says which it is. Two Bomb achievements are leaving the game, so they are not listed.

Still being finalised. Planned entries are not in the game yet and may change before they arrive. Runs in Gentle mode will not earn them. A few achievements already in the game are being tightened, so the game's own wording can differ for now.
