Rooms and goals

A room is rarely just "kill everything". The marks on a door tell you what is behind it, and the top wall tells you the rules once you are inside.

Room goals

GoalWhat to do
HuntAn elite is marked. Bring it down and the rest scatter: the room clears. Pays a reward and XP.
Hold outFoes keep coming. Survive until the toll ends: the room clears when the clock on the top wall runs out. Pays an item.
RaceClear the room fast: the sooner it clears, the more XP it pays. The bar on the top wall shrinks as time passes.
ThiefA thief runs off with a reliquary toward the far door. Catch it before it escapes for a reward and XP.
CrossingNo foes here, but the room's dangers run quick. Cross to the reliquary pad and hover on it. Pays XP and an item.
ReliquariesThree sealed reliquaries. Hover by one to break its seal: each starts its own wave and pays its own reward.
NestsNests on the walls keep sending foes. Shoot the nests down; the room clears with the last foe after them. Pays XP and an item.
WardsCandles shield every foe while they burn. Shoot the candles out. Put out every one for a reward and XP.
MotesMotes drift through the fight. Fly through them to catch them: each pays XP, all of them an item.
Trial: melee onlyYour guns are silent for the whole fight. Tap toward a foe to strike it with your melee weapon; the Parry still works. Clear every wave for a reward and XP.
Trial: no hitTake no hit through every wave. Keep it for a reward and XP. One hit breaks the trial: the room still clears, with no reward.
Trial: before the bellBeat the bell: clear every wave before the clock runs out; the clock is on the top wall. In time: a reward and XP. Late: the trial breaks and the room just clears.
Trial: parryCut the set number of shots with the Parry before the last of the waves falls; the count is on the top wall. Done: a reward and XP.
Trial: groundedNo dash in this room, and take no more than one hit through every wave. Keep it for a reward and XP.
Room rule: ricochetEnemy shots bounce once off the walls here. Watch the angles.
Room rule: tempoA bell keeps time here: every attack and hazard starts on its beat. Listen for the tick and move between beats.
Room rule: armouryA loaner gun for this fight. Your own gun is one tap of Switch away, and your guns come back when the room clears.
Surprise: the mirrorSomething wears your shape and fires your own gun back at you. Bring it down for a reward and XP.
Surprise: a memoryA Guardian you have beaten returns as a memory, weaker than before. Bring it down for a reward and XP.
Surprise: heavy shotsEvery enemy shot here is twice the size and slow. Weave between them and clear the room for a reward and XP.
Surprise: the turning weightThis room's weight turns: everything is pulled toward one wall, and the wall changes as the room turns. Clear it for a reward and XP.

Room leads

Most ordinary fights are led by one of the sector's two named leads: a bigger foe with a ring of runes at its feet, its name and health bar on the top wall, and three warned attacks of its own. Treat it as a small boss. Up to three helpers come with it, and the fight turns as it weakens.

Room forms

An ordinary fight may be laid out in one of thirteen forms: moats, islands, naves, cells and rooms that change as the fight goes on. The ones to learn:

  • Drops are floor to you, because you fly. They are walls to enemies that walk, and one knocked against the edge falls. Fliers and bullets cross freely.
  • Cracked stone can be shot down, by you and by enemy fire, so cover does not last.
  • Shifting rooms warn you with marks on the floor before they change.

Things in rooms

  • A bell on a post: strike it and the room's trap springs once. Use it on enemies.
  • A chandelier: shoot it to drop it where it hangs.
  • Censers: they blow up when shot and can hurt you too.
  • Sleepers ("zz"): strike them first for a bonus.
  • A falling reliquary: grab it within 8 seconds.
  • A chest: from sector 2, one chest a sector may be a mimic. It has tells, and pays more when it falls.
  • Enemy crossfire: shots that fly far enough hurt the enemies they meet.

Dares

A plain fight may offer one optional dare on its top wall, paying an extra reward:

  • Cut a set number of shots with the Parry.
  • Graze a set number of shots.
  • Land a set number of blade strikes.
  • Down a set number of foes with blasts.
  • Drop the chandelier on a foe.
  • Strike a set number of sleepers before they wake.

In a cleared room

  • Offering altar: hover by it for a free gift.
  • Bell of Defiance: makes your next fight harder, in exchange for double XP and a reward.
  • Cursed reliquary (from sector 2): a reward and an item now, but any hull hit fells you until ten kills or a fall ends the curse.
  • Vault: one side room a sector, behind a barred door. It opens with a Wardkey from the sector's elite, by arriving soon enough, or on a streak of twenty kills without a hull hit.

False walls and hidden bosses

False walls open only when you press against them. They stand off the middle of their side. In sectors 1 to 4 they glow. From sector 5 nothing marks them at all, and the Sector Complete banner counts the secrets you missed.

Behind them wait hidden bosses and supply caches. Every sector holds several, and from sector 2 one of them is an echo lair where the previous sector's Guardian returns. They are optional, but beating 2 of them opens the Guardian's door early.

The Wakeful

Linger in a sector for seven minutes without resting at a dock and a toll warns you, then an untouchable hunter enters your fights. It drifts through walls and costs a hull plate on touch. Keep moving, or rest at a dock.
