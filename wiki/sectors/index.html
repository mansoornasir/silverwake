Hazards can hurt enemies too

Lure an enemy onto a room hazard's live floor and it takes the sector's reaction: roots hold it in sector 1, the flood swallows enemy shots in 2, a bell stuns in 3, then frost, burn, gravity, spores, lightning, rockfall and shadow through to sector 10. This never works on a boss.
