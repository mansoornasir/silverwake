Skills

XP is the only currency in the game. Skills stay for every future run. Open the tree from the start menu, or from Pause during a run.

Earning XP

You do thisXP
Kill an enemy1
Kill an elite4
Clear a room3
Clear an ordinary fight without a hull hit4 more
Beat a Guardian40
Beat a hidden boss50

Trials pay extra for each better medal, and a Guardian beaten without a hull hit in its sector offers one more reward. See what a death does to your XP.

The tree

Each skill needs the one named beside it. The Starforged branch opens once the Last Organ, sector 10's Guardian, has fallen.
