Settings and accessibility

Everything here can be changed at any time from Settings. Only Gentle mode has any effect on your progress, as explained below.

Seeing and comfort

SettingWhat it does
Text sizeEvery screen's words
High-contrast threatsEnemy bullets stand out more
Reduced motionCalmer menus, banners and map
Reduced flashesSofter flashes and pulses
Boss dialogueWhat bosses and the dead say: first time, always or never
MusicMusic loudness
EffectsSound effects loudness

No critical warning in the game relies on colour, sound, bloom, particle count or tiny text alone.

Difficulty

SettingWhat it does
Gentle modeKinder fights, no achievements, from your next run
Jump scaresOnce a sector a room surprises you

Gentle runs stay at Vigil 0 and do not count for the Weekly Vigil, and they earn no achievements. Everything else about them plays and saves as normal.

Battery and heat

SettingWhat it does
Frame rate (fps)Max: the screen's full rate, smoothed. Lower keeps the phone cooler and the battery longer
Smooth motionOn: 90 and 120 Hz screens draw at their full rate, smoothed. Off: a steady 60 fps
Battery saverDraws at 30 fps
Detailed heroThe Vanguard drawn at your screen's own pixels
Phone running hot? Set Frame rate to 60 or 30 and turn off Detailed hero. These change only how the game is drawn. Hit areas and attacks stay exactly the same.
