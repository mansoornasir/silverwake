The game

You are the Lanternbound Vanguard, an armoured marine on suit thrusters, flying through ten haunted Gothic sectors laid out anew every run. Every enemy shows what it is about to do: learn the warning, step out of the line, and answer with your blaster and your blade.

  • One-thumb control. Drag anywhere to fly and aim; your blaster fires by itself. Tap to dash, or tap toward an enemy to dart in with your melee weapon.
  • Ten sectors, twenty bosses. A Guardian in every sector, a hidden echo lair where the last Guardian returns, secret rooms and secret bosses behind false walls.
  • Eleven guns, two carried, swapped with a two-finger tap; four melee weapons; a Parry that cuts bullets out of the air; twelve modifiers and twenty-seven relics to build around.
  • Lantern Docks save your run and refill your hull. XP buys permanent skills in a skill tree. Boss Arena and Boss Rush for every Guardian you have beaten.
  • Play together: two phones on the same Wi-Fi or a hotspot, no internet needed.
  • Plays offline. No account. Adjustable text size, reduced motion and flashes, high-contrast threats, battery saver.
